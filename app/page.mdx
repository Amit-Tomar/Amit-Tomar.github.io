# Hello, this is Amit Tomar.

Programmer • Human Being

From 5 hundred thousand employees, to 5 member companies, I have been fortunate to experience it all !

I was a masters student at [IIIT Bangalore](http://www.iiitb.ac.in/), India, where I had most fun attending Computer Graphics lecutres by [Prof. T K Srikanth](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tksrikanth) and [Prof. Jaya Sreevalsan-Nair](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jayanair/). This website serves as a platform to share information about me and some ideas via [blog](/blog).
